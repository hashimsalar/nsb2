About

A generalist who turns ambiguity into strategy.

I'm a generalist who gets to the frontier of hard problems fast. I just finished a B.S. in Social Sciences and Business at Minerva University, an interdisciplinary program built around complex systems, statistical modeling, and decision science, and I've spent five years turning messy, open-ended questions into research, strategy, and things that actually get built.

My work spans GIS and demographic analysis (redesigning a flood awareness campaign for 150,000 people), product and go-to-market (launching a supplement into a roughly $2.5B category), data-driven strategy (growing a region's university applications by 400%), and field research (mixed-methods homelessness policy in San Francisco). I learn a domain quickly, talk to the people who actually know things, and write the result up clearly. Now I want to bring that to AI safety and biosecurity field-building with BlueDot Impact, a nonprofit building the workforce that protects humanity, helping figure out what should exist, why it doesn't yet, and how to make it legible enough to get built.
