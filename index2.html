About

A generalist who turns ambiguity into strategy.

I just finished a B.S. in Social Sciences & Business at Minerva University — an interdisciplinary program built around complex systems, statistical modeling, and decision science. For the last five years I've spent my time turning messy, open-ended questions into research, strategy, and things that actually get built.

My work spans GIS and demographic analysis (redesigning a flood-awareness campaign for 150,000 people), product and go-to-market (launching a supplement into a ~$2.5B category), data-driven strategy (growing a region's university applications by 400%), and field research (mixed-methods homelessness policy in San Francisco). I get to the frontier of a domain fast, talk to the people who actually know things, and write the result up so it's clear enough to act on.

That's the exact muscle I want to bring to AI safety and biosecurity field-building. BlueDot Impact — a nonprofit building the workforce that protects humanity, with 6,000+ people trained and alumni at Anthropic, DeepMind, and the UK AI Safety Institute — is tackling the strategic clarity these fields are bottlenecked on. I want to help figure out what should exist, why it doesn't yet, and make it legible enough to get built.
